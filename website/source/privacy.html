Website privacy

A plain-language outline of how this website is intended to operate.

Browsing the website

The proposed portfolio does not require visitor accounts, contact forms or direct payments. Optional advertising and analytics scripts are not planned. These intentions must be checked against the deployed website.

Hosting and external stores

The hosting provider may process request information, including IP addresses, browser information and timestamps, to deliver and protect the site. Actual provider practices and retention still require review. External storefronts have their own privacy practices.

Product privacy

Games and software need separate privacy notices based on the exact distributed version. No app privacy policy is activated by this draft.

Privacy requests

Publisher identity, contact, retention and applicable rights procedures remain pending. Do not send personal information through this preview.
