# WEB-09.02B — Privacy and support readiness
2026-11-27 | Internal draft | Publication blocked

Extends CUSTOMER_SUPPORT.md and PRIVACY_STANDARDS.md; does not replace either. The website is a static portfolio, not a game server, support portal or payment processor. Cloudflare Pages is provisional.

## Public route plan
- /privacy.html: website-specific notice, separate from apps.
- /support.html: product directory and approved support instructions.
- Stable product-specific privacy and help URLs: add only when release-specific evidence and approval exist. Keep historical policy versions. Do not link an unfinished policy from a store.
- Existing Sudoku drafts in website/drafts are useful starting points; Beta 3 draft is explicitly not applicable to Beta 2. Do not silently substitute it.

## Website privacy draft (not effective)
Eden Practical is intended as an informational website for games, software and designs. The proposed site does not require visitor accounts, include contact forms, process payments directly or intentionally install advertising or analytics scripts. These statements are design intentions until checked against the deployed build. The hosting provider may process request information such as IP address, browser information and request timestamps to deliver and protect the site. Actual purposes, retention, recipients and applicable rights must be confirmed before publication. Links to external stores take visitors to services with their own privacy practices. If you contact support, information you choose to provide may be used to respond and investigate the issue. Separate product notices describe application-specific behavior. Do not interpret this draft as a promise of zero collection, no cookies, or no server logs.

## Support draft (not effective)
For an issue, identify the product, installed version, distribution channel, device and operating system, expected behavior and steps to reproduce. Optional screenshots should be sanitized. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, private messages, connection secrets or full backups. Back up progress where supported before destructive troubleshooting; do not uninstall to bypass update-signature errors. Use the applicable storefront for purchase and refund requests. Confidential security and privacy concerns require a verified private reporting route. Support is not an emergency service; no guaranteed response times.

## Owner decisions and release gates
1. Confirm actual publisher identity, jurisdiction, intended audiences and monitored contact. Prefer a single dedicated support inbox with confidential handling; no fabricated address, home address or public contact until approved.
2. Confirm hosting service terms, request logging, cookies, access controls, provider roles and retention; inspect actual deployed site and network requests. No optional analytics by default.
3. Define minimal private intake storage, access, retention/deletion schedule and rights-request process; review applicable obligations with qualified help where needed.
4. Confirm inbound/outbound contact and confidential reporting with synthetic messages. Test safe handling of normal bug, save loss, security report and refund referral. No real customer data for tests.
5. Reconcile every public app notice against exact distributed release, including existing Sudoku Beta 2; respect Eden Comms stricter security charter.
6. Verify approved stable HTTPS routes and links, mobile readability, keyboard and screen-reader access; owner approves final wording and effective date before publish.
