Installation and progress

Follow the release-specific instructions. Export progress where supported before updating. Do not uninstall or clear data to bypass an update-signature mismatch.

Preparing a report

Include product, version, distribution channel, device and OS, expected behavior and reproduction steps. Sanitize optional screenshots. Never include credentials, private messages, payment details or full backups.

Purchase and refund requests follow the relevant storefront process. Confidential security and privacy concerns require a verified private route. Support is not an emergency service.

Contact availability

A dedicated support contact is not available yet. For public release information and ordinary gameplay feedback, visit the itch.io listing linked from the relevant product page. Do not post private or security-sensitive information publicly.
