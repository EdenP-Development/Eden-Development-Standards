## Proposed player-facing wording (subject to verification)

The Last Taters stores garden progress, save slots and their recovery copies, Gardener profile information, scores, and audio and motion preferences in your browser's local storage so you can return to your game.

Deleting a garden save removes its primary and backup slot in the reviewed source. Other preferences and profile records may remain. Browser site-data controls can remove stored game data for the relevant origin; this may permanently erase progress and other data on that origin. Storage availability and retention depend on your browser and hosting context.

The game is distributed through itch.io. The hosting platform may handle information separately under its own privacy policy: https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy. Voluntary feedback and support are also separate from gameplay. Avoid posting private information publicly.
