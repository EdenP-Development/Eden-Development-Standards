# Eden Development Standards

Practical shared rules for PlayerEden games and applications. Keep development enjoyable, approachable and maintainable; use just enough process to prevent lost progress, lost keys and repeated work.

**Public repository: never store credentials, keystores, passwords, personal saves or private recovery archives here.**

Start with [Signing key management](SIGNING_KEY_MANAGEMENT.md), [release policy](RELEASE_POLICY.md), [app registry](APP_REGISTRY.json), [save preservation](SAVE_PRESERVATION.md), [release checklist](RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md) and [disaster recovery](DISASTER_RECOVERY.md). Use the [handoff template](templates/PROJECT_HANDOFF.md) when changing chats. Registry values marked unknown are not verified; never guess them.

## Current publishing workflow
Use [itch.io publishing guide](ITCH_PUBLISHING_GUIDE.md) for evergreen storefronts, honest devlogs, authentic screenshots, artwork exports and release handoffs. Sudoku Beta 2 is owner-reported published under its verified new permanent beta identity; public download verification, durable archive and reviewed source promotion remain pending. The original Beta 1 signing key remains unresolved but is not a blocker for the new beta channel. Consult the current app registry rather than historical status text.

Adopt these standards incrementally across projects. Do not change existing app identities merely to standardize names.

## Brand identity
Use [Brand identity and product attribution](BRAND_IDENTITY.md) for Spudacus, GridMates, EPS, EPD and Eden Practical. Adopt creator attribution incrementally without changing app identity or approved product artwork.

## Shared artwork workflow
Use [Original artwork pipeline](ARTWORK_PIPELINE.md) for asset provenance, original binary transfer, integration and evidence gates. A successful transfer is not equivalent to independent checksum or visual approval.

## Commercial strategy
Read [Commercial strategy and monetization (BUS-01)](MONETIZATION_STRATEGY.md) for owner-approved phased commercialization, paid-product preferences, customer safeguards and follow-on compliance milestones. This does not authorize storefront changes or paid releases.

## Intellectual property and commercial rights
Read [Asset licensing and IP readiness (BUS-02)](ASSET_LICENSING.md) and use the [asset rights register](templates/ASSET_RIGHTS_REGISTER.md) for provenance, commercial-use review, dependency obligations and brand screening. Technical or visual approval does not constitute legal clearance; verify rights before public distribution, including free betas.

## Privacy and data handling
Read [Privacy standards (BUS-03)](PRIVACY_STANDARDS.md) and use the [data handling register](templates/DATA_HANDLING_REGISTER.md) to document release-specific data, permissions, dependencies, storage, network behavior and verification. Stricter project security charters remain binding; this is not a published privacy policy or compliance certification.

## Commercial release readiness
Read [Commercial readiness (BUS-04)](COMMERCIAL_READINESS.md) and use the [commercial release acceptance receipt](templates/COMMERCIAL_RELEASE_RECEIPT.md) alongside the existing release checklist. Passing CI or completing branding does not establish paid-release acceptance; mandatory project gates and explicit owner authorization remain required.

## Google Play readiness
Read [Google Play readiness (BUS-05)](GOOGLE_PLAY_READINESS.md) and use the [Play release receipt](templates/GOOGLE_PLAY_RELEASE_RECEIPT.md). The policy snapshot is dated and must be rechecked before launch; no account creation, key transfer, pricing or publication is authorized.

## Customer support and maintenance
Read [Customer support and maintenance (BUS-06)](CUSTOMER_SUPPORT.md) and use the [support intake template](templates/SUPPORT_INTAKE.md) for proportionate triage, private reporting, refunds, maintenance and retirement planning. Actual contacts, customer-facing commitments and retirement decisions require owner approval.

## Business establishment and reinvestment
Read [Business establishment (BUS-07)](BUSINESS_ESTABLISHMENT.md) and use the [business readiness template](templates/BUSINESS_READINESS.md) privately for entity, tax, accounting and reinvestment planning. BUS-01 through BUS-07 establish the foundational business framework; actual compliance, product acceptance and owner decisions remain separate.
