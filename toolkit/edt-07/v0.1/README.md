# EDT-07 v0.1 — Autonomous simulation utility selection

Original dependency-free JavaScript ES module, independent of existing product implementations. Import decide(agent, opportunities) from decision.mjs. Agent fields hunger, fatigue, danger, curiosity, sociability and diligence are finite numbers in [0,1]. Higher hunger/fatigue/danger means more urgent need. Supply up to 256 tasks with unique ASCII IDs (1–64 characters, letters/digits/underscore/hyphen; idle reserved), kind eat/rest/safety/gather/work/explore/socialize, boolean available and normalized benefit/cost/risk. Caller computes these from a stable world snapshot and handles actor lifecycle.

Score = 70 * relevant drive + 30 * benefit - 20 * cost - 40 * risk. Gather/work use diligence, exploration curiosity, socialize sociability, survival tasks their corresponding needs. Eligible tasks compete by priority first, then score; equal scores resolved by lexicographic ID. Danger >=0.8 grants safety priority 3, hunger >=0.85 eat priority 2, fatigue >=0.85 rest priority 1. All others priority 0. Idle is always eligible at score 0. An unavailable emergency response cannot win. Emergency priority can outweigh any risk/cost penalty; caller MUST exclude unsafe or impossible tasks through availability, not simply assign high risk. Idle may win when no viable useful task exists; no guarantee of survival. Zero-score ties may favor an activity over idle. Defaults are provisional simulation design choices, not a balanced product model.

Returns version, selectedId, kind, reason and sorted trace (including unavailable tasks). Pure deterministic scoring, no mutation, randomness, learning, reservation, time progression, world consequences, pathfinding, task execution or multiplayer. Not ML. No actual social-need meter or skills yet. Do not treat the result as authorization to consume world resources. Revalidate and reserve at execution boundary.
