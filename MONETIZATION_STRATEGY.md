# BUS-01 — Commercial strategy v1.0

Status: owner-approved strategic direction, November 25, 2026. Individual prices, business formation, tax treatment, storefront changes and paid releases require separate decisions. This is internal development policy, not legal advice or a claim of incorporation.

## Principle
Earn the purchase rather than engineer the purchase. Build complete, useful, polished products first; price fairly; respect customers; keep operations proportionate to an independent developer's capacity.

## Phased approach
1. Development: free internal QA; prioritize reliability, usability, accessibility and maintainability.
2. Public betas: generally free or optional donations where the storefront supports them; explicit owner approval, honest beta labels and existing release gates still apply.
3. Commercial releases: prefer affordable one-time storefront purchases of complete products, without requiring in-app billing infrastructure.
4. Google Play: long-term expansion, not a current launch commitment. Verify platform policies, developer identity, testing, privacy, payment, refund, tax and territorial requirements at the actual release gate. Decide permanent listing monetization before publication; verify free-to-paid restrictions.
5. Growth: consider free products, advertising, optional ad removal, expansions or other purchases only when product fit, implementation capability, privacy and maintenance justify them. No automatic commitment.
6. EPS commercial software: consider individual purchases and business licensing; no mandatory accounts, cloud or subscriptions by default. Evaluate support and licensing obligations before offering commercial terms.

## Brand application
- Spudacus and GridMates: complete affordable paid games are the initial preference; free betas, demos, voluntary donations and bundles may be appropriate. Preserve game identity and avoid pay-to-win or intentionally frustrating mechanics.
- EPS: prefer paid, advertisement-free practical utilities. Local-first and minimal data collection remain design goals, subject to actual product evidence. Eden Comms retains all security and release gates; no sensitive-use guarantees.
- EPD: merchandise and digital designs; confirm commercial rights and marketplace terms.
- Eden Practical: umbrella identity; no assertion of registered business status.

## Engineering and customer safeguards
Do not integrate advertising, analytics, billing SDKs, license servers or mandatory accounts merely in anticipation of revenue. Use storefront-managed transactions where appropriate. Any exception requires owner approval, a privacy and dependency review, clear pricing, purchase restoration where relevant, testing, support and a maintenance plan. Avoid deceptive pricing, dark patterns and forced advertising. Respect existing purchases and access commitments; do not quietly remove promised functionality or force package/signing migrations. Document beta-to-paid transition, update entitlement and refund/support expectations before publication. No invented prices, revenue forecasts or sales claims.

## Commercial release gate
A paid release is a separate owner decision, not a consequence of passing CI or finishing branding. Require product-specific acceptance, verified distribution artifacts, accurate descriptions and limitations, compatibility/save checks, support contact, rights/provenance records, privacy/data inventory and applicable legal/storefront review. Follow RELEASE_POLICY.md, RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md, BRAND_IDENTITY.md and ITCH_PUBLISHING_GUIDE.md. Do not publish, change pricing, create accounts or register a business without authorization.

## Responsible growth
Keep business records separate from public source repositories; record gross sales, fees, refunds, expenses and net proceeds in private records. Evaluate taxes, reserves, hardware, QA, accessibility, legal and maintenance needs before discretionary reinvestment. No allocation percentages, entity choice or tax conclusions fixed by this policy.

## Project handoff
Read this strategy at new-project and release planning milestones. Continue current development; do not add monetization SDKs or change existing storefront pricing solely because of this policy. Record each proposed commercial model, decision status, dependencies and unresolved obligations in its project handoff. Bring genuine commercial decisions to the owner.
