# GridMates audio direction — provisional v0.1

2026-11-25. Owner direction: shared musical identity with distinct gameplay families. This is a design and rights checkpoint, not a released soundtrack.

- Cozy puzzle family: Sudoku and Word Search. Felt piano, acoustic guitar, restrained percussion, warm paperback nostalgia, low-distraction loops.
- Board/strategy family: Grid Hunt and Crossroads (proposed shared palette, pending audition and owner feedback). Light plucked strings, marimba, wooden percussion, measured rhythmic movement, playful strategy rather than sleepy study music. Avoid battle music, urgency or excessive repetition.
- Common series identity: warm acoustic instrumentation, understated motifs, optional silence; shared cues only where appropriate. No mandatory common track across all titles.
