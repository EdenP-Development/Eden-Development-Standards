Status vocabulary: Unknown; Review pending; Documented for specified use; Restricted; Blocked. Documented does not mean legal certification. Track visual approval and SHA-256 separately.

For AI-assisted assets add tool/provider, model if known, generation date, plan/terms evidence, input provenance, human contribution and transformation history. For dependencies include version, direct/transitive relationship and notice/source obligations. For fonts/audio/data include embedding or redistribution rights as appropriate. Group only identical rights conditions and link individual asset manifest.
